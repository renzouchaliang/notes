Good research notes make it possible to return to a question without reconstructing the whole investigation. This sample offers a starting structure for your own documents.

## Start with a question

Write down what you want to understand. Keep the question specific enough that new evidence could change your answer.

## Keep evidence close

Separate observations from interpretation. Record where a claim came from, when you accessed it, and what limitations apply. Link to an original source whenever possible.

```text
Question → Evidence → Interpretation → Next question
```

## Leave a useful next step

Finish with the unresolved question or the next document to read. A note can be useful before it is complete.
